What we build, and why it holds up

Every project starts underground — base prep, drainage, and grading — before a single visible stone goes down. That's the part clients don't see and the part that determines whether it lasts.

01

Patios & walkways

A patio that drains correctly, settles evenly across seasons, and doesn't heave or crack after the first hard freeze-thaw cycle. We compact a proper base before we even think about pattern.

02

Retaining walls

Engineered to hold a slope, not just decorate one — with drainage backfill and geogrid reinforcement sized to the wall's actual load, not a rule of thumb.

03

Outdoor kitchens & fire features

Built around how you'll actually cook and gather outside — gas lines and clearances handled up front, so the finished space works the first weekend you use it.

04

Landscape lighting

Low-voltage lighting that makes the yard usable after 6pm, not just visible from the window — path lighting, uplighting, and step lights on one control.
